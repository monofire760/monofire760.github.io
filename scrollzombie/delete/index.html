Delete your Scroll Zombie data

Scroll Zombie: Reels Blocker · Android app com.monofire.reels.shorts.blocker · Developer: Monofire Studio

In short

  • Scroll Zombie has no accounts. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your reel counts and settings live only on your phone.
  • Optional anonymous usage stats and crash reports are linked only to a random ID, which you can reset at any time.

1. Stop and reset anonymous usage data (in the app)

  1. Open Scroll Zombie and go to Settings.
  2. Turn off Anonymous usage stats.

This stops all collection by Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics, deletes crash reports that were not sent yet and resets the random analytics ID. Data sent before can no longer be linked to your phone.

2. Delete everything stored on your phone

Uninstall Scroll Zombie, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Scroll Zombie → Storage → Clear storage. This deletes all counts, history and settings at once.

3. Ask us to delete data

Email monofire760@gmail.com with the subject “Scroll Zombie data deletion”. We reply within 30 days. Because we hold no name or contact details, we may not be able to find data that is linked only to a random ID; in that case we will tell you, and step 1 resets that link.
