HeroVPN Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 5, 2026

Monofire ("we", "our", "us") operates the HeroVPN mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

Our core principle: HeroVPN does not log, monitor, or store your VPN traffic. We cannot see which websites you visit or what data you transmit through the VPN tunnel.

1. Information We Collect

Information we do NOT collect:

  • Browsing history or VPN traffic content
  • DNS queries
  • IP addresses of websites you visit
  • Personal information such as name, email, or phone number
  • Location data

Information collected automatically:

  • Crash reports: When the app crashes, we collect technical information about the crash (device model, OS version, stack trace) through Firebase Crashlytics. This helps us fix bugs and improve stability.
  • App usage analytics: We collect anonymous usage data through Firebase Analytics, such as which screens are viewed and how often the app is opened. This data is not linked to your identity.
  • Advertising data: Our app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect device advertising identifiers to serve relevant ads. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

Purchase information:

  • If you subscribe to HeroVPN Pro, your payment is processed entirely by Google Play. We do not have access to your payment details (credit card number, billing address, etc.). We only receive confirmation of your subscription status.

2. How We Use Information

  • Crash reports: To identify and fix bugs
  • Analytics: To understand app usage patterns and improve features
  • Advertising: To display ads and support the free tier of the service
  • Subscription status: To provide access to premium features

3. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics
  • Google AdMob

4. Data Security

Your VPN connection is encrypted using the WireGuard protocol, which provides state-of-the-art cryptographic security. All data transmitted through the VPN tunnel is protected from interception.

App data stored on your device (preferences, connection history) is stored locally and is not transmitted to our servers.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

All app data is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all local data. Crash reports and analytics data are automatically deleted after 90 days by Firebase.

6. Children's Privacy

HeroVPN is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
