Scroll Zombie · Android app · com.monofire.reels.shorts.blocker

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026 · Last updated: 30 October 2026

Summary

  • Scroll Zombie counts the Reels, Shorts and Facebook Reels you watch so you can set a daily limit. It uses Android's Accessibility API for this, only inside Instagram, YouTube and Facebook.
  • What is on your screen is processed only on your phone. It is never stored and never sent anywhere.
  • Your counts and settings are stored on your phone. There is no account, no sign-in and no advertising.
  • If you allow it, the app sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports to Google Firebase so we can fix bugs and improve the app. You can turn this off at any time in Settings.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, nothing is sent until you agree on the first launch.

1. Who we are

Scroll Zombie is developed and published by Monofire Studio, İstanbul, Türkiye (“we”, “us”). We are the data controller for the limited data described in this policy. Contact: monofire760@gmail.com.

2. Data that stays on your phone

  • Daily counts of Reels/Shorts per app (Instagram, YouTube, Facebook), which days were counted, and your streak and limit history. History older than 65 days is deleted automatically.
  • Your settings: daily limit, Remind or Lock mode, lock options, report and notification choices, app language, and your usage-statistics choice.

We have no server and cannot see this data. Like most Android apps, it may be included in the device backup that Android itself makes to your Google account, if you have turned on backup on your phone; that backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings.

3. Accessibility API: what it is used for

Scroll Zombie uses the AccessibilityService API because it is the only way for the app to notice that a new Reel or Short has appeared on screen. You turn it on yourself in Android Settings, after an in-app explanation and consent screen, and you can turn it off at any time.

  • It is limited to three apps: Instagram, YouTube and Facebook. It does not observe any other app.
  • Inside these apps it looks at the screen layout to tell whether a Reel or Short is showing, so the app can count it, remind you, or (only if you chose Lock mode) show a lock screen when your own daily limit is reached.
  • Screen content (text, video titles, account names, captions, comments, links) is processed only on your phone, never stored and never transmitted.
  • Only the resulting numbers (for example “12 Reels in Instagram today”) are kept on your phone. If usage statistics are on, anonymous daily totals and milestones such as “limit reached” are included in the analytics described in section 4.
  • Accessibility data is never used for advertising, never sold and never used to build a profile of you.

4. Data sent to Google Firebase (optional)

We use two Google services, Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, under one switch: Settings → Anonymous usage stats.

What is sent

  • App interactions: screens opened and actions taken in the app (for example onboarding steps, setting changes, limit mode, reports turned on or off).
  • App activity totals: daily number of Reels/Shorts per app, limit reached, reminders and lock screens shown, unlocks. Only totals and milestones, never per-video data.
  • Diagnostics: whether the counting service is running, setup-guide results, notification permission state.
  • Crash reports: the error and where it happened in our code, app version, device model, Android version, and whether the app was in the foreground.
  • Technical data collected automatically by Firebase: a random app-instance ID and a Crashlytics installation ID, device model, Android version, app version, language, and approximate country/region derived from the IP address. Google Analytics does not store the full IP address.

What is never sent

  • Anything read from your screen: video IDs, titles, captions, account names, links, comments.
  • Names of other apps you use.
  • The advertising ID (we removed this permission), Android ID, IMEI, phone number, email address, contacts or precise location.
  • A user ID or any text you type.

Why

To find and fix crashes, to see where setup fails on certain phones, and to understand which features help people watch fewer short videos. We do not use this data for advertising. Google Analytics advertising features are turned off: consent signals for ad storage, ad user data and ad personalisation are always set to denied, and the property is not linked to any advertising product.

5. Consent and legal bases

  • EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland: usage statistics and crash reports are collected only after you agree on the first launch. Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and the ePrivacy rules for storing data on your device).
  • Other countries: they are on by default, and you can turn them off in Settings at any time. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the app stable and improving it (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)), with minimal, pseudonymous data and an easy opt-out. For users in Türkiye, KVKK Art. 5(2)(f) (legitimate interest) applies, and explicit consent where required.
  • On-device data (counts and settings) is processed to provide the service you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)); it never leaves your phone through us.

How to withdraw consent or opt out

Open Scroll Zombie → Settings → turn off Anonymous usage stats. Collection stops immediately for both Analytics and Crashlytics, and the analytics ID on your phone is reset, so new data cannot be linked to earlier data. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time.

  • EEA/UK (GDPR): you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Because we have no account and data in Firebase is tied only to a random ID, we usually cannot identify you. The fastest ways to use your rights are:

  • Stop collection and reset your ID: turn off Anonymous usage stats in Settings.
  • Delete local data: uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android Settings.

If you are under the age at which you can give consent for online services in your country, please ask a parent or guardian before turning on usage statistics. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us data, contact us and we will delete it.
